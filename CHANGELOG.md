# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First version.

- Two decks with touch and Circle Pad scratching (hold L / R / both), CUE, play, SLIP, loops,
  hot cues, pitch faders and BPM sync.
- Mixer, EQ, Effects (Echo, Flanger, Crush, Gate), Sampler and Record pages.
- SELECT swaps the screens so the turntables can be played on the touch screen.
- Background analysis: waveforms, BPM and loudness (Auto Gain), with a BPM cache.
- Library from ID3 tags and `.m3u` playlists in `sdmc:/MP3`, with search.
- Settings, including saved deck colors with a reset to the default red and blue.
